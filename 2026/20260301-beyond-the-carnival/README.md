# Patras Tech Talk 2026.02 [Beyond the carnival]

**Date:** Sunday, March 1, 2026, 11:00 - 13:30 EET
**Venue:** Conference and Cultural Centre of the University of Patras, Seminar Room II-9 (first floor)

## Talks

### Leadership - Is it for me?
**Speaker:** Goga Lampropoulou
**Time:** 11:15 - 12:00

Senior Engineering Director, QA at Persado with over 20 years of experience across the defense, security, telecommunications, and software industries. Specializes in leading remote teams and fostering high-performance cultures.

The gathering welcomes attendees for icebreaker presentations, discussion, and interaction with the tech community of Patras. The program includes networking, with doors opening at 11:00 and closing at 13:30.
