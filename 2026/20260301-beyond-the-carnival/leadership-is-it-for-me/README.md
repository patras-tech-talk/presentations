# Leadership - Is it for me?

**Speaker:** Goga Lampropoulou
**Event:** Patras Tech Talk 2026.02 — Beyond the Carnival
**Date:** Sunday, March 1, 2026, 11:15 - 12:00 EET
**Venue:** Conference and Cultural Centre of the University of Patras, Seminar Room II-9 (first floor)

## Abstract

Do you have what it takes to turn the ship around?

Explore with Goga Lampropoulou what leadership looks like in real life and what it takes to lead. Besides lessons learned, she will share the tips and tools she still uses to navigate her own leadership journey.

## Speaker Bio

Senior Engineering Director with over 20 years of experience across the defense, telecommunications, and software industries. Specializes in leading global teams and fostering high-performance cultures that deliver innovative products and solutions.
