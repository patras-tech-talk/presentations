# Patras Tech Talk 2026.04 [AI && LN]

**Date:** Wednesday, April 29, 2026, 18:00 - 21:00 EEST
**Venue:** Innovation Hub Region of Western Greece, Πανεπιστημίου 151, Patras

## Talks

### Lightning Network: Open Money at the Speed of Light
**Speaker:** Vaios Karastathis
**Time:** 18:25 - 19:05

Explores the Lightning Network from an engineer's perspective: how the open protocol works, self-hosting options, and a live demo of instant payments.

### Project Management in the AI Era
**Speaker:** Christos Tselios
**Time:** 19:10 - 19:50

A talk on how project management changes as AI becomes part of modern software and product work.

## About

The gathering welcomes attendees for talks, discussion, and networking with the tech community of Patras. The program includes networking, with doors opening at 18:00 and closing at 21:00.

The event is hosted in the Innovation Hub of the Region of Western Greece, powered by the Industrial Systems Institute of Athena RC.
